## Initial Phishing Indicators

1. Urgent subject requesting account verification.
2. Sender presents itself as IT Support.
3. Email asks the recipient to use a verification link.
4. The link uses the simulated domain secure-login.example.test.
5. The message warns that access may be temporarily restricted.
6. No SPF authentication result is present.
7. No DKIM-Signature is present.
8. No DMARC authentication result is present.
9. No Received headers are available for mail-routing analysis.
